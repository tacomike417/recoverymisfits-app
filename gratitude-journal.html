About this tool

About the Gratitude Journal. This is a private journaling exercise and nothing more. It is not AI, and no person or program reads or responds to what you write. The questions are prewritten and the same for everyone. What you type stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

The Gratitude Journal is not counseling, therapy, or medical or professional advice, and it is not a substitute for your program, your sponsor, or professional care. Recovery Misfits is not a treatment provider.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US), any time, day or night. In an emergency, call 911.

The part nobody talks about
“I was to know happiness, peace, and usefulness, in a way of life that is incredibly more wonderful as time passes.” Bill W., Alcoholics Anonymous, p. 8

Sure, recovery means looking at where we were wrong. Inventory matters.

But the old-timers weren’t only digging up the bad stuff. They were asking something else, too: Where was I happy today? Where was I at peace? Where was I useful?

That part’s tucked right there in the Big Book, and hardly anybody sees it. It’s the stuff that gets you here:

“We have found much of heaven and we have been rocketed into a fourth dimension of existence of which we had not even dreamed.” Alcoholics Anonymous, p. 25

That’s a grateful mindset. That’s what we’re about to look at.
