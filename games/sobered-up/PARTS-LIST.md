# Sobered Up — character art

Decision (13 Oct 2026): the character is drawn in code (doll.js), vinyl-figure style, so sliders work —
skin and hair color are any color on a ramp, and hair length, beard length, build and height are sliders.
No PNG parts are needed. To change how he looks, change doll.js.

Room pieces (bed, dresser, TV, couch, bike, dog, car in the window) are also drawn in code, in roomSVG().
