# Spiritual Gangster v7 — "One Year" (plan, Mike's decisions 15 Oct 2026)

Goal: get your homie ONE YEAR sober. Win = the 1-year chip.

## Time speeds up
- Days 1–30: one DAY per turn (chapters 1–2, like now).
- Days 31–90: one WEEK per turn (chapter 3). Overnight wild cards become "this week" cards.
- Day 91–365: one MONTH per turn (chapters 4–5).
- About 48 turns total. Still ONE turn per real day (6 AM reset) → about 7 weeks to play.
- Still 3 moves a turn. Bigger turns = bigger moves ("work doubles all week", "meetings every night").
- Chips: 24h, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days, 6 months, 9 months, 1 YEAR.

## Danger
- Same overnight odds ALL year. Difficulty comes from life getting heavier, not cops getting meaner.
- Relapse: sober clock to 0, back to one-day turns until 30 days again, but he KEEPS his life (place, job, car, Jaz if she stays).
- Locked up: start over, chapter 1 (same as now).

## The squeeze (everything he gains comes with a bill)
- Car: payment + insurance every turn. Miss it → repo.
- Jaz/Marc: time + money (dates, birthday, meeting family). Skip her too long → trust drops.
- Own place: rent + utilities, higher than the sober house. Behind → lights shut off.
- Wreckage from the past shows up mid-year: court fees, child support, old debt.
- Time clash: a turn he works = NO meeting ("during your shift"). Call sober support still works.
- Hard decisions: a MIX — some situations still have a clean answer, more brutal two-bad-options ones as the year goes on.

## Chapter 5 (months 6–12) story threads
- Sponsoring Dre: he becomes the Pops. Dre's relapses and wins hit him too.
- Mending family: amends with Mom, the kid, or the ex.
- Career move: lead at the warehouse, or a trade.
- Starts a LANDSCAPING business that becomes super successful.
- Pops gets sick: the man who carried him needs carrying.
- Jaz gets pregnant (girl homie: she gets pregnant with Marc).
- A psychopath gets admitted to the sober house.

## Ending
- 1-year chip at a meeting: a cinematic — he takes the chip, Pops/Mom/Jaz/Dre in the room, his whole year replayed in texts. Then NEW RUN.
