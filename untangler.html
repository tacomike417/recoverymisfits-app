About this tool

About the Untangler. This is a private sorting exercise and nothing more. It is not AI, and no person or program reads or responds to what you write. The questions are prewritten and the same for everyone. What you type stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

The Untangler is not counseling, therapy, or medical or professional advice, and it is not a substitute for your program, your sponsor, or professional care. Recovery Misfits is not a treatment provider.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US), any time, day or night. In an emergency, call 911.
