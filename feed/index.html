Why I'm on the fence

I was raised in recovery on humility and anonymity. Principles before personalities. A feed full of faces and milestone posts could pull against that.

And this is a recovery app, not an AA app. My roots are AA, but anybody in recovery is welcome here, whatever their path.

A lot of people find recovery online now. Seeing real people make it could be the thing that keeps somebody going.

WHAT WORRIES ME

  • Anonymity. One photo could out somebody who never said OK.
  • Newcomers are raw. A feed could turn into drama, comparing, or somebody posting at their worst moment.
  • It's work. Reports, moderating, and a photo check all have to be there from day one.

WHAT I LIKE

  • Newcomers seeing real people with real time.
  • Celebrating coins and Survival Pile cards together.
  • Nobody having to feel alone at 3am.

HOW PHOTOS COULD WORK

  • Every photo gets checked for people before it posts.
  • Just you, or no people at all? It posts.
  • Anybody else in it? You have to confirm you're not breaking their anonymity, or that you have their permission.

So I'm asking people I trust before I build anything. Your vote counts.
