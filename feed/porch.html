THE PORCH RULES

We share experience, strength and hope.
Not opinion, strength and hope.

Opinions can hurt people, and in recovery we're not in the people-hurting business anymore. So the doctors get to be the doctors, the lawyers the lawyers and the counselors the counselors. Here, we're people in recovery helping each other out.

We're all adults here. Let's act like it.

  • Share what happened to you, not what somebody else ought to do. No medical, legal or counseling advice.
  • Be kind. We're all in this together.
  • Every path counts. No bashing anybody's recovery.
  • No hate, slurs or threats.
  • Protect other people's anonymity.
  • Say what you need. Somebody here gets it.
