# One Day Back — the script

*17 Oct 2026. Replaces the six scenes at the top of CUTSCENES.md. Same arc,
new voice: two sober pals, present day, happy to be here. An old phrase slips
out now and then ("dear friend," "the good book") and that's the only tell.
Innocent. Goonies. Two buddies find a problem and decide it's theirs to fix.*

*Each scene is a short video made the show way: ChatGPT stills, hard cuts,
Poppins captions, music, a sound or two. First frame carries the joke.
Nobody in a meeting is ever shown from the front — backs of heads only.*

**ONE DAY BACK**

## CS0 — The intro (~12s) — plays before the walking starts

**1.** Night sky over a small town. Porch lights. A paper pumpkin in a window.
> *Two old friends got one day back on Earth.*

**2.** Two men standing on a sidewalk in 1930s suits, looking at a glowing phone in a stranger's hand walking past. Wide-eyed, delighted.
> **BOB:** Everything's different.
> **BILL:** Everything's the same. Look.

**3.** Across the street, a church basement door, light spilling out. A folding sign: MEETING TONIGHT 7PM.
> **BILL:** They're still doing it.
> **BOB:** Dear friend. Let's go say hi.

*(cut to the walking game)*

## CS1 — AA, the recognition (~8s)

**1.** The doorway, from inside the room. Bill and Bob lit up in it, beaming. Rows of backs of heads turning.
> **BILL:** Evening, everybody.

**2.** Same doorway. Their smiles freezing. (Sound: a coffee cup hitting the floor.)
> **ROOM:** …

**3.** A chip rolling across the linoleum toward the camera. A man doubled over in the front row, back to us, hand up.
> **SOMEBODY:** I think I'm going to throw up.

**4.** Outside, the door shut behind them. Bill still smiling. Bob already walking.
> **BILL:** They remembered us.
> **BOB:** Too well. We need clothes.

## CS2 — Fresh Threads (~6s)

**1.** A thrift store curtain. A hand-lettered sign: EVERYTHING $5.
> *(sound: curtain rings)*

**2.** They step out in regular 2026 clothes — hoodie, jeans, ball cap, one of them in a puffer vest. Bill checking himself out in a mirror. Bob tugging at a hoodie string.
> **BILL:** How do I look?
> **BOB:** Like a guy named Dave.

**3.** Bob, hands in hoodie pocket, deciding he likes it.
> **BOB:** This has a pocket for your hands.

## CS3 — CA, the first one (~8s)

**1.** The doorway again, from inside. Nobody turns around this time. Bill is delighted. Bob is already scanning the coffee table.
> **BILL:** Not a single look. We're nobody.
> **BOB:** Where's the chocolate?

**2.** The coffee table. Coffee. Creamer. Napkins. A dented cookie tin, empty.
> **BOB:** Coffee. Creamer. Napkins.
> **BOB:** Where are the candy bars?

**3.** Bob holding up the empty tin like evidence. Bill patting his shoulder.
> **BILL:** Bob.
> **BOB:** The doctor said keep chocolate around. It's in the book.
> **BILL:** You're the doctor.
> **BOB:** I know what I said.

## CS6 — Harrison Corner, the turn (~12s)

**1.** An older room, Canton. They're on folding chairs, tired, hoodies rumpled. Bob is quiet for the first time.
> **BOB:** Five meetings. Two towns.
> **BILL:** Not one candy bar.

**2.** A kid's paper pumpkin taped to the wall by the coffee pot. Bill looking at it.
> **BILL:** Bob. What's today?

**3.** Bob looking up.
> **BOB:** …It's Halloween.
> **BILL:** The one day the whole world is handing out candy.

**4.** The two of them standing up at the same time. Bob picking up the empty cookie tin.
> **BOB:** That's not a coincidence.
> **BILL:** That's the job.

**5.** Title, Bangers:
> **GET THE CHOCOLATE BACK.**

*(cut to trick-or-treating)*

## CS7 — Midnight (~10s) — after the candy delivery

**1.** The same church basement door from CS0. A folding table just inside, heaped with candy bars. Backs of heads leaning in.
> *(sound: a room laughing)*

**2.** Bill and Bob on the sidewalk outside, looking in through the window. Bob holding the empty tin.
> **BOB:** The doctor was right.
> **BILL:** The doctor was right.

**3.** The two of them walking off down the sidewalk, away from camera, under the porch lights. The sky going from dark to a little darker.
> **BILL:** Same time next year?
> **BOB:** If they'll have us.

**4.** End card:
> **ONE DAY BACK**
> *Keep coming back.*

## Notes for making the pictures

- ONE Bill & Bob reference sheet first. Attach it to every prompt.
- CS0 and CS1 they're in the old suits. CS2 onward they're in regular clothes — hoodie, ball cap, jeans, puffer vest. Those clothes don't change again.
- Meetings: backs of heads only, no faces but theirs. Every prompt gets that line.
- No lettering in any picture. The captions go on in the edit.
- Vertical, 9:16.
- About 24 pictures total.
