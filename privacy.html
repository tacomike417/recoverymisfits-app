Privacy & Terms

Anonymity comes first here. This is everything the app keeps, and everything it never keeps, in plain words.

Last updated October 1, 2026.

PrivacyTerms

We never ask for your real name or phone number. We never sell anything about you, and there are no ads.

Privacy

Just using the app

No account needed. Your sober date, your journals (Burn Pad, Fear Compass, Gratitude Journal, Nightly Inventory) and your settings stay on your phone only. We never see them.

We use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages get opened, what kind of device, and a rough area like a state. It never gets your name or anything you write.

Your app account

A username and a password. No email, no phone number. Your sober date is saved to your account so it follows you to a new phone, and only you can read it. It's never on your profile.

The Porch

  • Your email: used once to send a 6-digit code, so one person gets one account. We scramble it and throw it away. Nobody, not even us, can read it back.
  • What you share: shares, photos, Spins, comments, hearts and friends are kept so people can see them. Your profile is Members only unless you switch it to Public.
  • Photos: checked by Google Cloud Vision for nudity and violence, and to find where faces are so you can confirm everybody said OK. It never works out who anybody is.
  • Spins: stored and played through Bunny.net, our video service.
  • Messages: only you and your friend can read a chat. Moderators can't. If you report somebody from a chat, only the reason you picked gets sent, never your messages.
  • Groups: only a group's members see what's shared in it, and who's in it. Moderators can look in on any group to keep it safe.
  • Find friends near me: off unless you turn it on. Your spot is rounded to about 7 miles and kept where nobody can read it, not even other members. People only see names and faces, never a place or a distance. Turn it off and it's deleted.
  • Phone alerts: if you turn them on, your phone's browser gives us an address to send alerts to. Turn them off any time.
  • Reports: if you report a share, comment or person, a moderator sees the reason and the thing you reported.

Who helps run it

Supabase (our database), Cloudflare (the website), Bunny.net (videos), Google (photo checks and visit counts) and Freesound (Spin music). They only get what they need to do that one job.

Deleting

Your account: on the Porch, open Edit profile, then tap Delete my account at the bottom. Everything goes for good: your username, the sober date saved to it, your settings, and your shares, photos, Spins, comments, messages, friends, saves and profile.

What's on your phone: clearing the app's data in your browser settings removes it.

Kids

The Porch is for adults 18 and older.

Terms

This is just an app

Recovery Misfits is one more way onto the road. It's not a treatment program, not therapy and not medical advice, and it doesn't replace meetings, a doctor or a counselor. If you're in danger, call 911. If you're having a hard time, call or text 988.

The deal on the Porch

  • You're 18 or older.
  • You follow the Porch rules: experience, strength and hope, not opinion. Be kind. Every path counts. No rated X behavior. No hate, slurs or threats. Protect other people's anonymity.
  • You only post photos and videos you have the right to share, and people in them said OK.
  • No selling, no spam, no scams.

What you post

It's still yours. You let us show it on the Porch to the people your settings allow. Delete it and it's gone.

When somebody breaks the rules

We can take posts down, pause an account while we look at a report, or remove somebody from the Porch. A real person makes those calls.

The fine print

The app is free and comes as it is. We work hard to keep it running and keep your stuff safe, but we can't promise it'll never go down or never have a bug. Things people post are their own words, not ours. If these terms change, we'll put the new date at the top.

Questions or something wrong? Use Report in the app, or message us on Facebook at facebook.com/recoverymisfits417.
